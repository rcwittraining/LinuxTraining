      About this simulator
      
      
      This page recreates the Anaconda installer used by Red Hat Enterprise Linux 10. It is an unofficial teaching tool. Nothing is installed on your computer.
      
      
        
        
      • Power & firmware — UEFI POST, then the DVD bootloader.
        • 
        
      • GRUB — Install, test media, or rescue. Use arrow keys and Enter.
        • 
        
      • Hub & spokes — Configure tiles on the Installation Summary, then Begin Installation.
        • 
        
      • First boot — GNOME if you picked a GUI environment, otherwise a text console.
